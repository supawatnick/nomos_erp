# Roadmap

The authoritative detailed delivery plan is [MASTER-PLAN.md](./MASTER-PLAN.md).

## Product cores
1. Finance & Accounting
2. Inventory & Warehouse
3. Procurement & Purchasing
4. Sales & CRM (including QT, sales recording and order tracking)

## Milestones
1. Phase 0 — Specification and architecture
2. Phase 1 — Engineering foundation
3. Phase 2 — SaaS platform core
4. Phase 3 — Catalog and warehouse master data
5. Phase 4 — Inventory engine
6. Phase 5 — Inventory Web ERP
7. Phase 6 — Inventory operations
8. Phase 7 — Business partners & CRM foundation
9. Phase 8 — Procurement & Purchasing
10. Phase 9 — Sales & CRM
11. Phase 10 — Approval & commercial controls
12. Phase 11 — Operational reporting
13. Phase 12 — Finance & Accounting
14. Phase 13 — LINE
15. Phase 14 — Commercial SaaS layer
16. Phase 15 — Commercial hardening

## Release gates
- Internal Inventory MVP: phases 0–6.
- Commercial Operations Beta: phases 0–11.
- **Core ERP V1: phases 0–12; all four ERP cores operational.**
- Integrated Channel ERP V1: phases 0–13.
- Commercial pilot: phases 0–15.
- GA follows pilot feedback/remediation.

No phase may bypass security, tenant isolation, auditability, data integrity, inventory authority or financial-posting boundaries.
