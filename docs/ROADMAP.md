# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Architecture, repository standards, local environment, CI, schema/migrations, auth and tenant model.

## Phase 1 — Inventory MVP
Products, warehouses, locations, ledger, receive, issue, transfer, adjustment, balances, history and dashboard.

## Phase 2 — LINE MVP
Account linking, signed webhook, stock queries, low-stock queries, confirmations and safe inventory requests.

## Phase 3 — Approval
Approval policies, manager actions from Web/LINE, notifications and audit.

## Phase 4 — Purchasing
Suppliers, purchase requests, purchase orders and goods receipt.

## Phase 5 — Sales
Customers, sales orders, reservations, picking and delivery.

## Phase 6 — AI Assistant
Natural-language tool calling, summaries and controlled operational assistance.

## Phase 7 — SaaS hardening
Billing/subscriptions, tenant provisioning, quotas, observability, backup/restore, security review and scale testing.
