## Release gates
- Internal Inventory MVP: phases 0–6.
- Web ERP V1: phases 0–11.
- Integrated ERP V1: phases 0–13.
- Commercial pilot: phases 0–15.
- GA follows pilot feedback and remediation.

No phase may bypass security, tenant isolation, auditability or data-integrity exit criteria.
