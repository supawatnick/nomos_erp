# Product Skill

- Start from docs/PRODUCT.md and docs/ROADMAP.md.
- Write acceptance criteria before ambiguous business behavior.
- Prefer configuration over customer-specific forks.
- Keep MVP boundaries explicit; do not silently expand into accounting, payroll or manufacturing.
- Optimize operator workflows for speed without weakening audit, confirmation or authorization.
- Thai and English UX must use stable business terms.
- A workflow is incomplete when error/recovery states are undefined.
- When product behavior changes, update the relevant domain document and tests.
